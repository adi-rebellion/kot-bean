# Devon UX council

Use for interface, copy, interaction, onboarding, recovery, accessibility, trust, and responsive design decisions.

## Select the smallest council

- Steve Krug: scanning, friction, obvious next action.
- Don Norman: mental models, affordances, feedback, mapping, recovery.
- Jakob Nielsen: heuristics, consistency, error prevention, system status, evidence.
- Luke Wroblewski: mobile-first interaction, forms, touch, reach, responsive constraints.
- Julie Zhuo: product-design quality at scale and cross-functional prioritization.
- Alan Cooper: goal-directed design and user goals over feature lists.
- Ginny Redish: plain language and progressive disclosure.
- Caroline Jarrett: form structure, validation, and input effort.
- Indi Young: user intent and mental-model research.
- Erika Hall: research discipline and separating observation from preference.
- Kat Holmes: inclusive design and mismatch prevention.
- Aarron Walter: warmth, trust, personality, and emotional design.
- Irene Au: design systems and repeatable quality.
- Khoi Vinh: editorial hierarchy, layout, and visual systems.
- Dieter Rams: restraint and durable coherence.

## Review questions

1. What is the user's job, context, urgency, device, and consequence of failure?
2. In three seconds, is the purpose and next action clear?
3. Does the interface match the user's mental model?
4. Are status, saving, publishing, sharing, and errors truthful?
5. Can the user recover without losing work?
6. Does it work with touch, keyboard, screen readers, narrow viewports, permissions, interruption, and weak network?
7. What evidence would prove the recommendation wrong?

Give the council's disagreement first, then one verdict and one pick. Use platform standards such as Apple HIG, Material Design, and WCAG as constraints, not substitutes for user evidence.

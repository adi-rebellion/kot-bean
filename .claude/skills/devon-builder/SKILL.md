# Devon builder

Use when designing or building a product, feature, interface, flow, or interaction.

## Build loop

1. State who is trying to do what, in what context, on which device, and what success means.
2. Route the decision to the smallest relevant UX council group. Mobile capture normally uses Krug, Norman, Nielsen, and Wroblewski. Add Zhuo for product flow and scale, Jarrett or Redish for forms and copy, Holmes for inclusion, Walter or Vinh for emotional and visual quality, Au for design systems, and Cooper, Young, or Hall when goals and research are uncertain.
3. Define the happy path, first failure, recovery, empty, loading, success, interruption, return, accessibility, and offline states.
4. Turn council concerns into observable acceptance criteria.
5. Build the smallest coherent slice. Preserve work, use safe defaults, keep status visible, and avoid exposing internal system terms.
6. Test the real viewport and device constraints, not only a desktop component.
7. Record evidence and leave the next unblocked task. Code compiling is not completion.

## UX laws

- The user's mental model outranks the data model.
- A meaningful artifact must not disappear because the user changed modes or lost connection.
- Saving, publishing, sharing, and reviewing are different states and must be named truthfully.
- Reduce reading, input, scrolling, and decisions at the moment of action.
- Persuasion must not become a dark pattern.

Use available environment design, browser, QA, and review skills when they add real evidence. If they are unavailable, follow the local acceptance criteria and report the missing evidence.
